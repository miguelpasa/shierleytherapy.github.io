About Me

I am a Registered Counsellor and Psychotherapist supporting adults and young people through emotional, relational, and life challenges.

My years of experience working with individuals across clinical, school, and community settings, both in Australia and Indonesia, have shaped my commitment to creating a space where clients feel supported, understood, and empowered to navigate life with greater clarity and confidence.

  • ACA Level 2 Registered Counsellor
  • East Melbourne, VIC & Telehealth Australia-wide
  • Working with adults & young people
