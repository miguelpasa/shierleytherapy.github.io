How I Can Help

Support that's tailored
to you.

Whether you prefer to meet face to face or connect from the comfort of your own space, support is available in a way that works for you.

What I
Offer

Individual counselling and psychotherapy delivered in a warm, confidential, and supportive environment.

Individual Counselling & Psychotherapy

A dedicated space to explore your thoughts, emotions, and experiences with support, curiosity, and care. Together, we'll work towards greater understanding, resilience, and meaningful change at a pace that feels right for you.

In-Person Sessions

Available in East Melbourne, VIC — offering a calm, private, and comfortable space where we can meet face to face.

Telehealth Sessions

Secure video sessions are available for clients anywhere in Australia, allowing you to access therapy from the comfort and privacy of your own space.

Areas of Support

Anxiety & Stress

Persistent worry, overthinking, or feeling overwhelmed by emotions in daily life.

Relationship Challenges

Navigating conflict, communication difficulties, or recurring patterns in romantic relationships, friendships, or family.

Life Transitions & Adjustment

Feeling lost, stuck, or uncertain during significant life changes, new chapters, or crossroads.

Self-Esteem & Confidence

Persistent self-doubt, inner criticism, or difficulty believing in your own worth.

Trauma & Past Experiences

Unresolved experiences or wounds from the past that continue to shape how you feel and relate today.

Burnout & Emotional Exhaustion

Feeling chronically depleted, disengaged, or overwhelmed by the demands of work and daily life.

Cultural Identity & Intercultural Dynamics

Navigating multiple cultural identities, family expectations, or the tension between where you come from and where you are.

Getting started

How we work
together.

  1. 01

    Book a free 15-min intro call

    A 15-minute phone call to briefly discuss what brings you here, ask any questions, and see whether working together feels like the right fit.

  2. 02

    Initial session

    Our first session is an opportunity to explore what brings you to therapy, understand your background and goals, and begin building a shared direction for our work together.

  3. 03

    Ongoing sessions

    Together, we'll work towards the goals that matter most to you, building insight, skills, and practical strategies to help you navigate challenges and create meaningful change in your life.
