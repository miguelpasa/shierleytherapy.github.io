Services

Introductory Call

Before your first session, I offer a free 15-minute introductory call to give us both the opportunity to connect briefly, discuss what has brought you to therapy, and explore whether working together feels like the right fit.

There is no obligation or commitment required — it is simply a chance to ask any questions you may have and get a sense of how I work.

The introductory call is conducted via phone or video and can be booked directly through the scheduling link on this website.

In-Person Sessions

In-person sessions are available in East Melbourne, VIC. Further location details will be provided upon booking confirmation.

Appointments are available by booking only.

Telehealth Sessions

Telehealth sessions are conducted online and are available to clients located anywhere in Australia.

This option may be suitable if you prefer the flexibility and comfort of attending counselling from your own space, or if in-person sessions are less accessible.

To support an effective session, it is recommended that you have:

  • a stable internet connection
  • a private and quiet space
  • a device with a working camera and microphone

Cancellations & Rescheduling

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please provide at least 48 hours' notice.

Late cancellations or missed appointments may incur the full session fee as outlined in the informed consent and client agreement.

If you have any questions or would like to discuss which option may be most suitable for you, please feel free to get in touch.
