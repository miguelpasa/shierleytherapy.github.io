# #12 Add Second Hero CTA + Move Phone to Header

**Priority:** HIGH
**Status:** `done`
**Effort:** Low — 1 hr
**Impact:** High (conversion)
**Tags:** SXO, CONVERSION

## Problem

The only above-fold CTA ("Discover") scrolls to an educational "What is Therapy?" section. Users arriving from "counsellor East Melbourne" already know what therapy is — they need a booking path, not an explanation. Crisis-adjacent and decision-stage users have to scroll past educational content before finding a way to contact Shierly.

## Fix

1. Add a second primary CTA in the hero: **"Book a free 15-min call"** linking to `cal.com/shierly-therapy`
2. Keep "Discover" as a secondary/ghost button
3. Move the phone/SMS number to the header or hero area so it's visible above the fold on every page
4. Consider embedding a cal.com inline widget on `contact/index.html`
