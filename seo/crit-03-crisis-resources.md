# #3 Add Crisis / Emergency Resources Sitewide

**Priority:** CRITICAL
**Status:** `done`
**Effort:** Low — 20 min
**Impact:** Critical (YMYL)
**Tags:** CONTENT, YMYL

## Problem

Google's 2025 Quality Rater Guidelines explicitly expect health/wellbeing pages to direct users to emergency services. A counselling site serving clients with trauma, anxiety, and burnout with no mention of Lifeline, Beyond Blue, or Triple Zero is the most significant YMYL trust failure on this site.

## Fix

Add the following text to the **global footer** (all pages) and the **Contact page**:

> "If you are in immediate distress or danger, please call Lifeline on **13 11 14** or Triple Zero (**000**). Counselling is not a crisis service."
