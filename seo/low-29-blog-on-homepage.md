# #29 Surface Blog Posts on Homepage

**Priority:** LOW
**Status:** `done`
**Effort:** Low — 1 hr
**Impact:** Medium
**Tags:** SXO, INTERNAL LINKING

## Problem

No "Latest from the blog" section exists on the homepage. Blog posts are only accessible from the nav. Surfacing posts on the homepage provides fresh content signals and cross-links topical content to the primary conversion URL.

## Fix

Add a "From the Blog" section to `index.html` with 2–3 post cards linking to blog articles. Update manually as new posts are published.

Best placed after the Process or FAQ sections, before the footer CTA.

## Files

`index.html`, `assets/css/styles.css`
