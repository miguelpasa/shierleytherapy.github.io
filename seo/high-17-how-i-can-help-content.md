# #17 Expand How I Can Help Page — Add Fees, Modalities, More Content

**Priority:** HIGH
**Status:** `todo`
**Effort:** Medium — 3 hrs writing
**Impact:** High
**Tags:** CONTENT, SXO

## Problem

The How I Can Help page has ~380 words — severely under-content for the primary commercial page. Top-ranking competitors run 1,800–2,800 words per condition page. Ten areas of support are each described in a single sentence inside collapsed `<details>` accordions. Therapy modalities (CBT, ACT, attachment theory, IFS) are never named. Fees are buried in the FAQ.

## Fix

1. Expand each area of support to 2–4 sentences minimum
2. Add a **"My Approach"** section naming at least 3 therapeutic modalities:
   > "I draw on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), attachment-based and Internal Family Systems (IFS) approaches, adapting to what is most useful for you. IFS training is currently in progress."
3. Add a **"Fees"** section with all three price points:
   - Standard session: **$150**
   - Saturday session: **$170**
   - Concession rate: **$100**
   - Note: no GP referral required
4. Aim for 800+ words total (long-term target: 1,500+)
5. Long-term: create dedicated pages for anxiety and burnout — these terms structurally cannot rank from a single section
