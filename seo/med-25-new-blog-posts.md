# #25 Publish 2–3 New Blog Posts

**Priority:** MEDIUM
**Status:** `todo`
**Effort:** High — 2–3 days writing
**Impact:** Very High (authority)
**Tags:** CONTENT, AUTHORITY

## Problem

A single blog post does not establish topical authority. Google's authority assessment for YMYL sites depends on breadth and depth of published content. The blog is listed in the nav but currently offers no evidence of content expertise.

## Fix

Publish 2–3 new posts, each 1,500+ words with a named author byline and Article JSON-LD:

1. **"What is anxiety counselling and is it right for me?"**
   Targets: "therapy for anxiety Melbourne", "anxiety counselling East Melbourne"

2. **"How to prepare for your first therapy session"**
   Targets: first-session intent queries; reduces friction for new clients

3. **"Understanding burnout: signs and what to do"**
   Targets: "burnout counselling Melbourne", "burnout therapy"

Each post should:
- Be 1,500+ words
- Include a visible "Written by [Full Name], ACA Level 2 Registered Counsellor" byline
- Include Article JSON-LD
- Be linked from the homepage "Latest from the blog" section (task [#29](low-29-blog-on-homepage.md))
