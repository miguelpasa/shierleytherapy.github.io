# #9 Claim and Optimise Google Business Profile

**Priority:** CRITICAL
**Status:** `todo`
**Effort:** Medium — 1 hr + verification wait
**Impact:** Very High (Local Pack)
**Tags:** LOCAL SEO

## Problem

No GBP link, Maps embed, place_id, or review CTA exists anywhere on the site. Without a claimed and optimised GBP, the practice will not appear in the Google Map Pack for "counsellor East Melbourne" or similar queries — regardless of all other SEO work.

## Fix

1. Claim/verify at business.google.com
2. Set primary category: **"Counselor"**
3. Add: phone (`0435 557 712`), website (`https://shierlytherapy.com.au`), service area (East Melbourne + inner suburbs + telehealth Australia-wide)
4. Upload 5+ photos including a professional headshot
5. Add session fee information
6. Add a **Google Maps embed** to `contact/index.html`
7. Add a **"View on Google"** link in the footer of all pages

## Notes

Postcard verification may take 1–2 weeks. Start this task early. Once live, add the GBP URL to the `sameAs` field in the LocalBusiness JSON-LD (task [#7](crit-07-json-ld-schema.md)).
