What is
Therapy?

Therapy is a collaborative space to explore your thoughts, feelings, and patterns — and to make sense of what you are experiencing. It is not just a space to talk, but an opportunity to understand yourself more deeply, shift how you relate to others, and find new ways of moving forward. You do not need to be in crisis to begin.

FAQ

Good things to know.

Still have a question? Get in touch — happy to help.

How do I get started?

You can begin by booking a free 15-minute introductory call to briefly discuss your needs and determine whether working together feels like the right fit.

How do I book, reschedule, or cancel?

You can book directly through the booking link on this website.

A minimum 48 hours' notice is required for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or missed appointments may incur the full session fee.

What services do you offer?

I offer individual counselling and psychotherapy for adults and young people, available through in-person and telehealth sessions.

How long is each session?

Standard counselling sessions run for 50 minutes.

What can I expect during a session?

Counselling sessions are collaborative and tailored to your needs. Sessions generally involve exploring your current concerns, thoughts, emotions, and patterns, while working towards greater understanding, clarity, and practical ways of moving forward.

There is no expectation for you to have everything figured out or know exactly what to say. You are welcome to bring whatever feels important, and we will work at a pace that feels comfortable and manageable for you.
